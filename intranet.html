Intranet

An intranet is a private, internal network that uses Internet technologies to share information and resources exclusively within an organization. It is completely inaccessible to the general public.

What is an Intranet?

An intranet functions like a private, internal version of the Internet used exclusively within a company, school, government department, or any other organization. It uses the same technologies as the public Internet — HTML, HTTP, web browsers — but is protected behind firewalls so that only authorized employees or members can access it.

Think of it as a company's own private website that the outside world cannot see or access under any circumstances.

Common Uses of an Intranet

  • Company News and Announcements: Sharing internal updates, policies, and notices with all employees
  • Document Management: Storing, organizing, and sharing internal files, reports, and forms
  • Employee Directory: A searchable database of all staff members and their contact details
  • HR Portals: Leave applications, payslips, benefits information, and HR forms
  • Internal Communication: Messaging boards, forums, and team collaboration tools
  • Training and E-Learning: Online courses, manuals, and onboarding materials for new employees
  • Project Management: Task tracking, timelines, and team collaboration spaces

Benefits of Using an Intranet

Improved Communication: Employees across different departments and locations can share information quickly and efficiently without relying on email alone.

Increased Productivity: Staff can find documents, policies, and tools in one central location instead of searching through multiple systems.

Enhanced Security: Sensitive company information stays within the organization, protected from outside access by firewalls and authentication.

Cost Savings: Reduces the need for printed documents and physical notice boards by providing a digital alternative.

Intranet vs Internet vs Extranet

Internet: A global public network accessible to anyone in the world with a connection.

Intranet: A fully private network restricted to members of one specific organization only.

Extranet: An extension of the intranet that selectively grants access to trusted outside parties such as suppliers and clients.
